 IMMUNOPHENOTYPING
SAMPLE: Bone marrow.
METHODOLOGY: A panel of monoclonal antibodies was directly conjugated with fluorescein
isothiocyanate (FITC), phycoerythrin (PE), peridinin chlorophyll protein complex (PerCP), PerCP-Cy5.5,
allophycocyanin (APC), APC-H7 tandem, PE-Cy7 tandem, Horizon V450 and V500. Data acquisition
and analysis were performed using a FACSCanto II flow cytometer with FACSDiva and Infinicyt
software.
RESULTS: Based on membrane immunophenotype, relative cell size, and internal complexity, the
following populations were identified: Total lymphocytes: 2.7%. Plasma cells: <0.1%. Monocytes: 3%.
Neutrophils: 21.3%. Eosinophils: <0.1%. Basophils: <0.1%. Mast cells: <0.01%. Nucleated erythroid
cells: 46.9%.
PATHOLOGICAL CELLS: 26.1% myeloid blasts of medium size. CD45+, CD34+, HLA-DR++, CD117+,
CD38++, CD13++, CD16(-), CD11b(-), CD10(-), CD71-/+, CD64(-), CD14(-), CD36(-), CD105(-),
CD33+, CD15-/+, CD56(-), CD7-/+(42%).
OBSERVATIONS: Neutrophils show marked dyshematopoietic features in maturation patterns CD13 /
CD16 / CD11b / CD10. Monocytes show a predominance of immature forms (85% of the total monocytic
lineage) with partial CD13 expression. The erythroid lineage is increased, with heterogeneous
expression of CD36 and CD71.
CONCLUSIONS: Presence of a myeloid blast population (26.1%) similar to those found in previous
studies. A final diagnostic conclusion requires correlation with the clinical presentation and additional
complementary studies.
